Why a Kids Ratha Yātrā?

A festival where children become the priests of joy

Every kīrtana led, every cup of cool lemonade poured, every fruit offered — this festival is the children's gift to Lord Jagannātha.

The Kids Ratha Yātrā is a special celebration where the children themselves take the lead — offering their talents, creativity and hearts to the Lord. Every stall, every dance, every cupcake offered is a small act of devotion. The grown-ups simply encourage.

This festival gives children the opportunity to:

  • learn devotion through service
  • develop confidence in Kṛṣṇa consciousness
  • offer their talents to Lord Jagannātha
  • experience the joy of devotional association

Come and encourage the children as they offer their love to Lord Jagannātha with enthusiasm and devotion.

Programme Schedule

Saturday, 27 June 2026 · 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

All timings are approximate (SGT).

6:00 – 6:10 PM

Assembling kids and parents

Families gather on the 1st floor of ISKM Capark.

6:10 – 6:20 PM

Welcome speech by HG Visvambhar Prabhu

A short opening address to set the spirit of the evening.

6:20 – 6:30 PM

Deities brought down to the chariot Conch Call

Samarth blows the conch shell as Lord Jagannātha, Baladev and Subhadrā are placed on the chariot.

6:30 – 6:35 PM

Dance by Kṛṣṇa Bhaktins Performance

Young Kṛṣṇa Bhaktins offer a devotional dance.

6:35 – 6:50 PM

Ārati, kīrtana, coconut breaking & sweeping by HG Mahāprabhu

Lamp offering, congregational chanting, breaking of the coconut and ceremonial sweeping of the chariot path.

6:50 – 8:45 PM

Pulling of the chariot Peak Moment

Nearly two hours of joyful chariot-pulling, kīrtana and stalls. Every family takes a turn on the rope.

8:45 – 8:50 PM

Final ārati & group photos

Closing ārati and family photographs with the chariot.

8:50 – 9:00 PM

Deities brought back up

The deities return to the altar with kīrtana accompaniment.

9:00 – 9:15 PM

Cleaning & prepping for prasādam

A short pause as the prasādam line is set up.

9:15 PM onwards

Prasādam feast begins Free Feast

Sanctified vegetarian feast for the whole family — come hungry, leave blessed.

Frequently asked questions

Registration is free and helps us plan seating, prasādam and goodie bags for the children. Walk-ins are welcome but we strongly recommend registering in advance so we can save a spot for your family.

All ages — but the festival is especially designed with children aged 3–13 in mind. Toddlers, teens and grandparents are all warmly welcome. The whole family can take part.

No strict dress code — modest, comfortable clothing is perfect. Traditional Indian attire is lovely but not required. Children participating in kīrtana or dance may wish to wear traditional dress. Please remove shoes before entering the temple hall.

Yes! A full vegetarian prasādam feast is offered free of charge from 9:15 PM. Throughout the evening you can also enjoy fresh samosas, cupcakes, cool drinks and other treats at the festival stalls.

Limited parking is available at ISKM Capark. We recommend using public transport. We'll send detailed directions in your confirmation email.
